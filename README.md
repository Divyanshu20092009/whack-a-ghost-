## How to Play

Click PLAY to start the game....
You can choose, if 45 sec gameplay or 60 sec gameplay
- White ghost: +1 point
- Golden ghost: +3 points
- Pumpkin: -2 points
- Blue ghost: Adds 3 extra seconds
- Combo: Every 5 successful hits in a row gives 2 bonus points....

Try to get the highest score before time runs out... and then try again

## keyboard controlss

- Click or tap the ghosts.
- Keys 1 to 7 Hit the seven holes....
- P or Escape Pause or resume the game.....

## Features

- Three difficulty levels: Easy and Normal and Hard.
- Different types of ghosts.... golden, blue  . white
- Combo bonuses and extra time.....
- if you don't like sound. you can turn off the music
- Best score saved in your browser....
- you will be able to see accuracy and all the other performance in last
